# Selling on Amazon Globally While Sourcing from China — A Complete Roadmap (2025–2026)

*A practical, sequenced guide from "open a company" to "first sale" and global expansion. Compiled from multi-source research; key facts cited. This is general research, not legal/tax advice — confirm the tax and tariff items with a cross-border accountant before you commit money.*

---

## TL;DR — The recommended path

1. **Model:** Start with **one private-label product via Amazon FBA**. It's the only model that fits "buy generic from China, brand it, sell globally," and the only one that builds a defensible asset.
2. **Where to start selling:** **Amazon US first** (largest market, simplest tax via marketplace facilitator), then expand to **Europe/UK** and other regions once you have a proven product. "Global from day one" is operationally hard for a first product — sequence it.
3. **Company:** A **US LLC** (Wyoming or New Mexico) is the most popular and tax-efficient vehicle for most non-US sellers — **unless you're a UK/EU tax resident**, where a US LLC creates a tax-classification conflict and a UK Ltd or home-country company is often cleaner.
4. **Budget:** Plan **~$5,000–$12,000** for a proper first private-label launch (lean is possible at ~$3,000, but that usually understates freight, tariffs, and a cash buffer).
5. **Biggest 2025–2026 watch-items:** (a) US **de minimis is gone** — every shipment is now dutiable; (b) **tariffs on Chinese goods are high and volatile** (~35% effective on most consumer goods as of mid-2026); (c) **Amazon ended its US FBA prep/labeling service (Jan 1, 2026)** — prep must now happen upstream in China; (d) **EU GPSR** now requires an EU-based "Responsible Person."

## Stage 0 — Decide your structure and model

### Business model comparison

| Model | Capital | Margin | Time to profit | Fit for China sourcing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FBA Private Label** | $3k–$12k+ | 25–40% | 3–6 mo | ★ Best — buy generic, brand it |
| Wholesale | $1k–$3k | 10–20% | 30–60 days | Poor — buys existing brands |
| Retail/Online Arbitrage | ~$500 | varies | fast | Poor — not from China |
| Dropshipping | $500–$1k | lowest | fast | Hard to do compliantly |

**Why private label:** China sourcing is built around buying unbranded goods at factory prices and applying your own brand — exactly the private-label model. It's the only path that qualifies you for Brand Registry / A+ content and the highest margins.

**Amazon's dropshipping rules (if tempted):** Dropshipping is allowed *only if you are the "seller of record"* — your name on all packing slips/invoices/packaging, you handle returns, and you remove any third-party drop-shipper references. **Buying from another retailer (Walmart, AliExpress, etc.) and having them ship directly is prohibited** and triggers suspension. ([Amazon Drop Shipping Policy](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G201808410))

### Jurisdiction: where to register

- **US LLC** — Non-residents can form one with just a registered agent, business address, and formation docs (no SSN, no US visit). Pass-through entity: generally **no US federal income tax** if there's no US-source "effectively connected income." Top states: **Wyoming** (low cost, privacy, $60 annual report), **New Mexico** (~$0 ongoing, strongest privacy), **Delaware** ($300/yr franchise tax; preferred if you'll raise investment). ([Globalfy](https://globalfy.com/blog/amazon-us-seller/), [Entity.inc](https://www.entity.inc/blog/best-state-us-llc-non-residents/))
- **⚠️ UK/EU residents, beware:** HMRC and several EU tax authorities treat a US LLC as "opaque," creating an entity-classification mismatch and double-taxation risk — you can be personally taxed on undistributed LLC profits. For these owners a **UK Ltd** (19% corp tax, US-UK treaty) or a **home-country company** is often the better choice. ([Sterlinx](https://sterlinxglobal.com/company-for-amazon-sellers-complete-guide/))
- **Net:** US LLC is the default winner for most non-residents *outside* the UK/EU-mismatch countries. Decide based on **where you personally are a tax resident** — confirm with a cross-border CPA.

> **⚠️ Genuine unresolved debate:** Whether a non-resident's FBA inventory sitting in US warehouses constitutes a "US trade or business" (creating US tax) is **disputed**. Older view: no. Conservative 2025 view: storing + continuously selling/restocking may create effectively connected income, and the IRS may be enforcing more aggressively. Many advisors recommend protective filings. Get professional advice before relying on a "no US tax" position. ([SellerCPA](https://www.sellercpa.com/blog/selling-with-amazon-fba-for-non-us-residents-the-definitive-guide/), [NVinc](https://nvinc.com/the-complexities-of-u-s-taxation-for-non-resident-amazon-sellers-why-opinions-differ/))

## Stage 1 — Form the company and get operational (Weeks 1–4)

Sequence (for a US LLC as a non-resident):

1. **File Articles of Organization** with the state + appoint a **registered agent** (mandatory in your formation state). ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/resources/more/how-to-open-an-llc-in-the-usa-for-nonresidents), [Northwest](https://www.northwestregisteredagent.com/llc/non-citizen))
2. **Operating Agreement** — internal doc defining ownership/management; banks require it. ([Slash](https://www.slash.com/blog/non-resident-llc))
3. **Get an EIN** (the business tax ID — needed for banking, Amazon, taxes). No SSN/ITIN required. Online tool won't work without an SSN, so non-residents apply by **fax or mail with Form SS-4** (or phone +1-267-941-1099 for international applicants). On SS-4: Line 7b = "Foreign"/"N/A"; Line 9a = "Foreign-owned U.S. Disregarded Entity." Fax turnaround ~4 business days. Keep the **CP 575** confirmation letter. ([IRS SS-4 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4), [Nonresident.tax](https://nonresident.tax/blog/ein-for-foreign-owned-llc-guide))
4. **ITIN** — *not* needed to form the LLC or get the EIN. Only relevant later for personal tax filings/treaty claims.
5. **Business bank account** — see options below.

### Banking for non-residents

| Provider | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Wise** | Easiest approval (~24h), ~$31 one-time for USD details, strong multi-currency |
| **Payoneer** | Best for direct Amazon payouts |
| **Mercury** | Real US bank account, but **tightened in 2025**: no registered-agent addresses, country restrictions, more rejections for brand-new entities with no revenue |
| Relay, Airwallex, Lili | Work for non-residents; open 2+ for redundancy |

Documents typically needed: formation docs, EIN (CP 575 or 147C letter), passport for any ≥25% owner, and a **real business address** (not a registered-agent address or PO box). ([Mercury docs](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28770957425172-Gathering-your-documents), [LLC University](https://www.llcuniversity.com/foreigners/open-us-bank-account-llc-non-resident/))

### Annual compliance you can't skip
- **Foreign-owned single-member US LLCs must file Form 5472 + a pro forma Form 1120 every year**, even with zero tax. Due **April 15**, by **mail or fax only** (no e-file). **Penalty for missing it: $25,000.** ([LLC University](https://www.llcuniversity.com/irs/form-5472-foreign-owned-llc/), [IRS Form 5472](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472))
- State fees: Delaware $300/yr; Wyoming ~$60/yr; New Mexico ~$0.

**Cost at this stage:** Formation ~$100–300 + registered agent ~$50–125/yr. Year-1 total often ~$125–300, plus accountant fees for the 5472/1120.

## Stage 2 — Trademark + Amazon account (run in parallel with Stage 1)

### File a trademark early
Brand Registry needs a **registered OR pending** trademark (a pending mark via Amazon's **IP Accelerator** qualifies). File it now so it's ready when your goods land. The trademark is what turns a generic import into a defensible brand and lets you lock out listing hijackers. Budget ~$250–350+ for filing. ([Brand Registry requirements](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/brand-registry-requirements))

### Open Amazon Seller Central
- **Professional plan: $39.99/month** (needed for Buy Box, ads, and most Global Selling) vs Individual ($0.99/item). ([Amazon pricing](https://sell.amazon.com/pricing))
- **Verification (2025):** government photo ID + recent proof of address, bank/credit card, and a **live video verification call** with an Amazon associate — complete within ~14 days or the account is deactivated. ([ecomclips](https://ecomclips.com/blog/documents-required-to-start-selling-on-amazon-2025/))
- You must reside in / be registered in an [Amazon-approved country](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G200405020).

### Global Selling structure (for later expansion)
- **North America unified account:** US, Canada, Mexico (one login). **Europe unified account:** UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, NL, SE, PL, BE. Japan/India/Australia/UAE/KSA are separate. ([Global Selling](https://sell.amazon.com/global-selling))
- **Build International Listings (BIL)** auto-translates and price-syncs listings across marketplaces.
- **Getting paid:** Amazon Currency Converter (~1.5% + FX spread → ~2.5–4% effective) vs cheaper third parties (Wise ~0.35–0.6%, Payoneer). ([ACCS](https://sell.amazon.com/programs/amazon-currency-converter))

> **Sequencing advice:** Even though you want to sell globally, launch in **one marketplace (US)** first. Validate the product, then use the unified accounts + BIL to expand. Each new region adds VAT/compliance overhead (below).

## Stage 3 — Source the product from China (Weeks 2–8)

### Where to find suppliers
- **Alibaba** — the standard beginner start: English, escrow (Trade Assurance), export-ready suppliers.
- **1688** (Alibaba's domestic site) — **10–30% cheaper** but Chinese-only, local payment, domestic shipping only → needs a **sourcing agent**.
- **Global Sources / Made-in-China** — curated, strong for electronics.
- **Sourcing agents** — typically **3–10% commission** (or $100–500/order); useful for 1688 access, factory visits, and consolidating freight. (Their savings claims are self-interested — verify.) ([zignify](https://zignify.net/blog/alibaba-vs-1688-yiwu-market/), [Cosmo Sourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/china-sourcing-agent-guide))

### Vet the supplier (critical — avoid scams)
- **Understand the badges:** *Verified Supplier* = third-party-inspected capability; *Gold Supplier* = **just a paid membership** (not a quality guarantee); *Trade Assurance* = escrow protection. **No badge proves they're the actual factory.** ([Cosmo Sourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/what-are-alibaba-verified-suppliers))
- **Factory vs trading company tells:** check the Chinese business-license name (贸易 = "trading"); verify the 18-digit license free on the government site **gsxt.gov.cn**; request a **live video factory tour**. ([Supplyia](https://www.supplyia.com/verify-chinese-supplier/))
- **Payment safety:** Pay **only on-platform** (>60% of scams involve off-platform payment). The bank account name **must match the business license** — a personal-name account is a red flag. ([SourcingArts](https://www.sourcingarts.com/pay-china-suppliers-avoid-scams/))

### Samples, MOQs, negotiation
- **Order samples from 2–3 suppliers** — a $50–100 sample can save $5,000 of bad inventory. Sample fees are often **credited against your first bulk order**. The approved sample becomes the **"golden sample"** inspectors judge against. ([Maple Sourcing](https://www.maplesourcing.com/how-to-manage-your-sample-orders-in-china.html))
- **MOQs:** typically 150–1,000 units; ODM/private-label often 300–500; logo-only jobs can be 50–100. **Lower MOQ by:** paying a bit more per unit, requesting a trial order, dropping custom packaging ("MOQ without custom packaging?" often cuts it 50%+), or framing as a long-term partnership. ([ChinaSourcingAI](https://www.chinasourcingai.com/blog/how-to-negotiate-moq-china-suppliers))
- **Payment terms:** standard is **30% deposit / 70% balance after pre-shipment inspection** (keeps the factory honest). T/T has no buyer protection — use Trade Assurance for new suppliers. ([QualityInspection.org](https://qualityinspection.org/pay-chinese-suppliers-tt-payment/))
- **OEM vs ODM:** **ODM** (existing product + your branding/packaging) is the common, faster FBA path; OEM = from-scratch design. ([JingSourcing](https://jingsourcing.com/b-oem-vs-odm/))

### Quality control
- Book a **pre-shipment inspection** (when 80–100% produced, ≥80% packed) via **QIMA, SGS, Bureau Veritas, or V-Trust**. Cost ~$200–350/man-day (SGS higher). Inspectors use **AQL** sampling (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4): typically 0% critical / 2.5% major / 4.0% minor defects. ([QIMA](https://blog.qima.com/quality-control/qima-sgs-preshipment-inspection-comparison))

## Stage 4 — Ship it and clear customs (Weeks 6–12)

### Incoterms (who pays/risks what)
- **EXW** — buyer does everything from the factory door (max responsibility). **FOB** — supplier loads it on the ship + clears export; you control international freight. **CIF** — FOB + supplier-arranged freight/min insurance. **DDP** — supplier delivers everything incl. duties.
- **Recommendation:** **FOB + your own freight forwarder** is the consensus best practice for FBA — best balance of cost, control, and transparency. (DDP is easiest for absolute beginners but pricier and risky for US import-of-record.) ([trade.gov Incoterms](https://www.trade.gov/know-your-incoterms), [King-Hor](https://king-hor.com/incoterms-for-china-importers-fob-exw-ddp-cif-explained/))

### Shipping method
| Method | Cost | Speed | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Express courier (DHL/UPS) | highest/kg | days | <100–150 kg, launches |
| Air freight | $4.50–12/kg | 2–7 days | restocks, time-sensitive |
| Ocean LCL | $0.30–0.80/kg | +days | ~0.5–15 CBM |
| Ocean FCL | $0.20–0.50/kg | ~25–42 days | 15+ CBM |

### US customs essentials
- **You (your company) are the Importer of Record** — foreign companies can use a **CBP-Assigned Importer Number** instead of a US EIN. Get a **customs bond** (single-entry for first shipment, or continuous/annual). File **ISF "10+2"** for ocean shipments ($5,000 penalty if missed). Classify with correct **10-digit HTS codes**. ([BorderBuddy](https://borderbuddy.com/blog/importer-of-record-explained-for-us-imports), [CBP](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/basic-import-export))
- Use a **freight forwarder experienced with Amazon FBA** — they book freight, handle docs/labeling, and deliver to fulfillment centers; they usually have an in-house **customs broker**. ([Threecolts](https://www.threecolts.com/blog/freight-forwarders-customs-brokers-amazon-fba/))
- **⚠️ Amazon will NOT be your importer of record or consignee** and won't provide a Power of Attorney or its tax ID. Naming Amazon as consignee = shipment refused. ([FreightFBA](https://freightfba.com/amazon-fba-customs-clearance/))

### EU/UK customs
- Need an **EORI number** (separate **GB** one for the UK and an EU-member one for the EU; **XI** for Northern Ireland). UK now requires Safety & Security declarations on EU→GB imports (from Jan 31, 2025). VAT-registered businesses can use **Postponed VAT Accounting** to avoid paying import VAT at the border. ([EU EORI](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/customs-procedures-import-and-export/customs-operations/economic-operators-registration-and-identification-number-eori_en), [SimplyVAT](https://simplyvat.com/eori/))

### ⚠️ Tariffs & de minimis — the volatile, must-check part
- **De minimis ($800 duty-free) is ELIMINATED** — for China/HK since **May 2, 2025**, and **globally since Aug 29, 2025**. Every package, any value, is now dutiable with full customs declaration. ([CBP fact sheet](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/factsheet_suspension_of_duty-free_de_minimis_treatment.pdf))
- **Tariffs on Chinese goods are high and changing.** As of mid-2026 the effective rate on most Chinese consumer goods is **~35% (10% Section 122 + 25% Section 301)**; List 4A items (apparel/footwear) ~17.5%. (Background: the Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs on Feb 20, 2026; a 10% Section 122 tariff replaced them on Feb 24, 2026 — but Section 122 is capped at 15% / 150 days and faces further challenge.) **Verify the live rate for your specific HTS code before you order** — this can be larger than your freight cost and decides whether the product is profitable. ([Baker Donelson](https://www.bakerdonelson.com/trade-policy-shifts-ieepa-tariffs-end-section-122-begins-and-sections-301-and-232-activity-grows), [CRS](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/LSB11398))

### FBA prep (big 2026 change)
- **Amazon discontinued its US FBA prep/labeling service (effective Jan 1, 2026).** FNSKU labeling, polybagging, and packaging must now be done **upstream** — at the factory or a China/US prep center. ([Unicargo](https://www.unicargo.com/blog-amazon-fba-prep-requirements-2026/))
- Each unit needs a scannable **FNSKU** label covering any existing UPC; boxes ≤25"/≤50 lb; poly bags ≥5" need a suffocation warning.
- **Direct China→FBA is allowed but risky** (label errors, missed ISF, undervalued invoices, country-of-origin marking). Many sellers route the first shipment through a **3PL/prep center** to de-risk. ([FreightFBA](https://freightfba.com/how-to-ship-from-china-to-amazon-fba/))

## Stage 5 — Compliance for selling (especially as you go global)

### VAT (the thing new sellers miss)
- **The moment you store inventory in an EU or UK FBA warehouse, you must register for VAT there — zero threshold, before your first sale.** ([SimplyVAT](https://simplyvat.com/blog/amazons-pan-european-programme-2/), [Sterling & Wells](https://sterlingandwells.com/blogs/uk-vat-registration-for-overseas-companies/))
- For low-value imports, Amazon acts as **"deemed supplier"** and collects VAT: EU consignments ≤**€150** (via IOSS) and UK ≤**£135**. This doesn't remove your own registration duty where you hold stock. ([amavat](https://amavat.eu/vat-for-marketplace-sellers-in-the-eu-all-obligations-in-one-place/))
- **OSS** lets you report intra-EU cross-border B2C sales in one return once you exceed **€10,000/yr**.
- *(Flag: the EU €150 exemption is being phased out toward ~2028.)*

### US sales tax
- **Amazon collects and remits sales tax** in all marketplace-facilitator states (effectively all). A pure Amazon-only FBA seller has little day-to-day work, but **FBA inventory creates physical nexus**, so you may still need to register in some states; **off-Amazon sales (your own site) aren't covered.** ([Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/learn/guides/state-by-state-guide-to-marketplace-facilitator-laws.html))

### Product safety / certifications (depends on category)
- **EU GPSR (in force since Dec 13, 2024):** all non-food consumer products need a designated **EU Responsible Person** physically in the EU, named on packaging/docs. No grace period; Amazon removes non-compliant listings and **ended its own RP service**, so you must source one. ([eucertify](https://eucertify.com/gpsr-responsible-person-deadline/))
- **CE (EU) / UKCA (UK)** marking for regulated categories; **FCC** for wireless electronics; **CPSC/CPSIA + Children's Product Certificate** for kids' products; **FDA/MoCRA** for cosmetics/food/supplements. ([CPSC](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Testing-Certification/Childrens-Product-Certificate), [FDA MoCRA](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/modernization-cosmetics-regulation-act-2022-mocra))
- Amazon may request **test reports** (QIMA/Intertek/SGS), **SDS** sheets (batteries/chemicals), or category approval.

## Stage 6 — Launch, then expand globally

1. **Create the listing** — keyword-researched title, professional photos, A+ Content (once Brand Registry is active).
2. **Enroll in Brand Registry** — unlocks A+ Content, Brand Store, Sponsored Brands, and anti-hijacker protection. ([Brand Registry](https://sell.amazon.com/brand-registry))
3. **Run PPC** to get initial sales/reviews; budget $500–$2,000 for launch.
4. **Reach profitability** — typically **3–6 months**; keep a cash buffer.
5. **Expand:** replicate into Canada/Mexico (NA unified account), then Europe/UK (handle VAT + GPSR), using Build International Listings.

## Things to verify before you spend money (fast-moving / disputed)

1. **The current tariff rate for your exact HTS code** — it's high, volatile, under active litigation, and can make or break the product's economics.
2. **Whether your FBA setup creates US income tax** as a non-resident — get a cross-border CPA's opinion.
3. **The best company structure for *your* tax residency** — US LLC is great for many, wrong for UK/EU residents.
4. **Amazon's current FBA prep and verification policies** — several changed in 2025–2026; confirm in Seller Central.
5. **VAT registration triggers** before storing stock in any EU/UK warehouse.

*All figures and policies reflect 2025–2026 research and will continue to change — treat the citations as starting points and confirm current details with the primary sources and a qualified advisor.*
